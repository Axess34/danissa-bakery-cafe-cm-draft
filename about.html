Our story · เรื่องราวของเรา

Slow-fermented. Fresh from the oven.

Danissa Bakery and Cafe sits on Ratchaphruek Road in Nong Khwai, Hang Dong, with a garden-front café and outdoor tables.

Our bread is slow-fermented and baked fresh, and our kitchen turns it into hearty brunch plates, comfort food and sweet treats.
